# FishPipe Cookie Policy

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how FishPipe uses cookies and similar technologies. FishPipe is a native Android application and does not operate a website-based advertising or analytics system.

## 1. Cookies in FishPipe

FishPipe does not set cookies for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking. The app does not include a third-party analytics SDK that places tracking cookies on your device.

## 2. Local storage

FishPipe uses local storage on your device for app functionality, including settings, download history, cached images, and temporary files. This local storage is not a cookie and is not used to track you across other apps.

You can clear cached images and temporary files from the Privacy screen. You can clear download history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app removes app-local storage, subject to Android backup settings.

## 3. Third-party websites

If you open a source page, creator profile, sound page, hashtag page, or other third-party link in a browser, that website may set cookies or similar technologies. Those cookies are controlled by the third-party site, not FishPipe. Review that site's cookie and privacy policies for details.

## 4. Resolver services

If you configure a custom resolver URL, requests are sent to that service. The resolver may use cookies or other technologies under its own policy. FishPipe does not control those practices.

## 5. No advertising cookies

FishPipe does not use advertising cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not sell cookie data.

## 6. Managing cookies

Because FishPipe does not use tracking cookies, there are no in-app cookie consent controls for advertising. You can manage cookies set by third-party websites through your browser settings. You can clear FishPipe's local cache from the Privacy screen.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy. The updated version will appear in the app with a new "Last updated" date.
