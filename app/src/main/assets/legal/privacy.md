# FishPipe Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how FishPipe handles information when you use the app. FishPipe is designed as a local utility: we do not operate an advertising network, analytics service, or user account system.

## 1. Information we process

### Links and media requests

When you paste, share, or copy a supported link, FishPipe processes that link to resolve media. The link and related request data are sent to the source platform and, if you configure one, to your own resolver endpoint. We do not receive a copy on FishPipe-operated servers.

### Download history

FishPipe stores download history on your device, including title, creator, caption, hashtags, media identifiers, source links, file location, file size, status, and timestamps. You can clear this history from the app. Clearing history does not necessarily delete downloaded files.

### Settings

FishPipe stores preferences on your device, such as language, theme, default download quality, folder organization, network limits, notification choices, and any resolver URL or key you enter.

### Images and media

FishPipe may download thumbnails, covers, videos, images, and audio to display or save content you request. These files are stored locally on your device.

### Diagnostics

FishPipe does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Basic app errors may be shown locally. If you contact support, you choose what information to include in your message.

## 2. Permissions

FishPipe may request:

- **Internet and network state** to resolve links and download media.
- **Storage or media access** to save and open files.
- **Notifications** to show download progress and completion.
- **Set wallpaper** if you choose the wallpaper feature.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. Denying a permission may disable the related feature.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used to:

- Resolve links and download the media you request.
- Display history, creator libraries, and media details.
- Remember your settings and preferences.
- Show download progress and completion notifications.
- Provide optional insights based on public data.
- Diagnose problems you report to support.

## 4. Sharing and disclosure

FishPipe does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only:

- To the source platform or your configured resolver when needed to fulfill your request.
- To service providers you choose to use, such as an external browser or media player.
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the app, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Third-party platforms have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before using their services.

## 5. Cookies and tracking

FishPipe does not use cookies for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not include advertising identifiers or third-party tracking SDKs. If you open a third-party page in a browser, that website may set its own cookies. See the Cookie Policy for more information.

## 6. Data retention

Download history and settings remain on your device until you clear them, uninstall the app, or delete the relevant files. Files saved outside the app remain until you remove them. Resolver services you configure may retain request data under their own policies.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information processed by the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, downloads, and resolver credentials.

## 8. Children

FishPipe is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. Do not use the app to download or share content involving minors in violation of applicable law.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. Because FishPipe stores most data locally, you can exercise many choices directly:

- Clear download history in the app.
- Delete downloaded files with your file manager.
- Change or clear resolver settings.
- Revoke app permissions in Android settings.
- Uninstall FishPipe to remove app-local data.

You may contact **theuncoaffair@gmail.com** with privacy questions or requests.

## 10. International processing

If you use a third-party platform or resolver, your request may be processed in countries other than your own. Those services are responsible for their own international data transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version will appear in the app with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes may be highlighted in the app.
