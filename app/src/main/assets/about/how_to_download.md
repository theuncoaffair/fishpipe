# How to download

## Copy a link
1. Open TikTok and tap **Share** on a video, photo slideshow, or profile.
2. Tap **Copy link**.

## Download in FishPipe
1. Open FishPipe and paste the link into the box on the home screen.
2. Tap **Download**.
3. Choose video, audio, or photos and pick the quality you want.
4. Tap the download button and wait for the file to finish.

## Share directly to FishPipe
1. In TikTok, tap **Share**.
2. Choose **FishPipe** from the share sheet.
3. Pick your download options and start the download.

## Find your files
- Open **History** in the bottom bar to see finished downloads.
- Tap a finished video, photo, or audio item to open it.
- Use the menu on a history item to share it, open its folder, or delete it.

## Tips
- Use **More download options** in a player to download a different quality.
- If a file already exists, choose **Redownload** to download it again.
- Creator downloads keep their files organized in creator folders when that setting is on.
